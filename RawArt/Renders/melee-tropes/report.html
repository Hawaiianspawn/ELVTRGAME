melee-tropes -- variant family

Trope-seeded, each spending a different silhouette axis: samurai (topology -- wide hanging sleeves plus a long curved blade angled up behind the shoulder), gladiator (asymmetry -- one oversized pauldron and a net/trident pair, the two sides deliberately unlike), riot-trooper (solidity -- a tall rectangular shield covering the whole front, near-rectangle primitive), monk (aspect narrow -- bare-handed, robes tight, a vertical bo staff, the slimmest form), headsman (topology top-heavy -- broad hooded shoulders and a greataxe head above the skull), duelist (asymmetry via a cape swept off one shoulder plus a thin rapier held out level, wide-by-extension). Every variant appears as rendered and again as a flat outline, because the outline is what survives at panel scale. All figures share one zoom, so relative size is true.

The opening — and the zone model that carries it

Five minutes, no tutorial text. You start one layer behind the fighting and move outward to reach it — the only time in the game the war goes that direction.

The route

Out and down to the Great Gate, then back in and up. Every beat teaches one thing by showing it.

The one rule that keeps this honest — a boss's marks must be earned identically in all three states. A front resolving in the Cold ledger has to produce the same marks the Live sim would have, or A9's consequence is a lie and players find out the first time a distant fall yields an unmarked boss.

What the measurements don't include — flat field, no level geometry, no navmesh, no gates, no audio, single client. The shape of the argument survives; the specific milliseconds are a floor, not a forecast.

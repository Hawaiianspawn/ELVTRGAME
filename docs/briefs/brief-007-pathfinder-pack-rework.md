## Subject

The Pathfinder's retinue ("the Pack": hawk, hounds, trained scouts — `CLASSES.md`
§3) is rebuilt from scratch alongside the hero rework in brief-005: not inherited
royal-hunt stock, but strays — human and animal survivors of the Fall who found
each other in the ruins and kept each other alive. No sprite work exists yet for
individual Pack members (the prior art pass, brief-004, specced only the hero), so
this is new scope rather than a correction — flagging it now so it isn't lost.

Named members introduced by the new fiction (companions keep proper names; only
the four player classes are nameless): the hawk **Relay** (a former Legion
signal-bird, not a hunting hawk — her old service designation kept as her name);
the hounds **Latch** and **Ash** (feral mongrel descendants of Legion war-dogs, not
royal-kennel breed stock); human **scouts**, recruited/grown from fellow
war-orphans the Pathfinder found or was found by.

## Mood

Scrappy, mismatched, survived-together — the opposite visual story from "matched
hunting pack out of a noble kennel." Nothing about this group should read as bred
or trained-from-birth; everything should read as picked up, patched up, and kept.

## Narrative excerpt

> A Legion signal-hawk, unflown since her handler's post went hollow, found a
> child worth following before she found one worth returning to. Two war-dogs,
> bred for a kennel that no longer fed them, worked out that a starving kid with a
> knack for finding grubs and blind fish was better company than starving alone.
> […] The hawk is Relay — her old Legion designation kept as a name on purpose:
> nobody ever called her anything before the Pathfinder did […] The hounds are
> Latch and Ash — not royal stock, just survivors, thick-coated mongrel
> descendants of Legion war-dogs gone feral generations back.

## Readability needs

- Must still satisfy the class's existing horde-scale readability rule: the Pack
  reads as *motion* — darts, high per-unit animation budget, few sprites
  (`CLASSES.md` §3 2-bit readability). That rule is unchanged; only the *why they
  look the way they look* changes.
- Relay should read as ex-military hardware gone feral, not a noble falconer's
  bird — a visual cousin to Still Legion silhouette language (per Legion animal
  stock) without reading hostile, the same "friendly-wearing-enemy-adjacent-shape"
  problem brief-001 solved for Warden-Captain Bree and brief-004 flagged for the
  Vanguard's own Legion-adjacent silhouette.
- Latch and Ash should read as a matched *pair* (they're referenced as a set) but
  visually scruffier/more mismatched than a bred hunting-hound line would be —
  asymmetric markings, patchwork coats, not a uniform breed silhouette.
- Human scouts need a visual language that reads as "grown up feral together," not
  as generic ranged-unit reskins — consider carrying some of the Pathfinder hero's
  own read (improvised gear, no formal uniform) down into the scout tier, since
  they share an origin story with the hero specifically.

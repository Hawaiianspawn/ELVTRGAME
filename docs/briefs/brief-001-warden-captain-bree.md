## Subject
The last officer of the un-hollowed Legion, holding one Highgates stairwell for
years against her former comrades. A middle-aged career soldier in Legion-pattern
armor she has patched herself and stripped of the crown sigil. Appears in three
gameplay contexts: at her post (NPC, flag N1 = met), marching with the party as an
elite recruit (recruited), or absent (post held / fallen).

## Mood
Tired discipline, not glory — procedure said like a prayer. She is warm the way a
kept watch-lamp is warm against Legion administrative-cold; the same uniform
language as the Still Legion, but *lived-in* where theirs is immaculate.

## Narrative excerpt

> down, do it clean."

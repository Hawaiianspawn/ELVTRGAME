## Subject

The Lampbearer's fiction changed substantially (owner decision, 2026-07-11) and the
existing sprite/portrait spec (`../art/noll.md`, delivered under brief-004, status
`done`) is now stale — it specs a 28-year-old male foundling. This brief supersedes
brief-004's Lampbearer portions and should feed a fresh pass on `../art/noll.md`.

New: female, mid-twenties. She does not carry her own lamp — she carries her dead
mentor's, and her mentor's soul-flame is captured inside it, an ongoing aware
presence, not a silent artifact. No proper name for the hero or the mentor, per the
2026-07-11 no-names decision (see `../narrative/noll.md`); the mentor is referred
to only as "her mentor" throughout.

The lamp itself is visually the same *object class* as the old Foundling Lamp
(flame + halo + wisp points, the shared one-flame rule, never guttered including
hero-down) — that visual grammar carries over unchanged. What changes is who she
is and her relationship to it: not a miracle child bringing up a mystery light, but
a young attendant carrying on a job that was handed to her mid-catastrophe, by
reflex, not by anyone's choice.

## Mood

Gentle, unhurried, unsettlingly unafraid — carried over from the old read — but
younger, and with an undertone of a private, ongoing conversation happening at her
hip that other people can't hear (the mentor's voice, in-fiction, is heard by the
player/party via bark lines, not necessarily by other NPCs — treat as a design
question for whoever specs the audio/bark system, not resolved here). Not haunted
in a horror register — tragic, not edgy, per tone rule 1: this is grief and duty,
not a ghost story.

## Narrative excerpt

> The Lampbearer does not carry her own lamp. She carries her mentor's — the one
> who trained her, worked the Vesper Halls for decades before her, and died with
> that lamp in reach. When it happened, the lamp did what a Lampbearer's lamp
> always does: it found the nearest unclaimed soul-flame and caught it, before
> anything worse could. The nearest flame was her mentor's own […] The Lampbearer
> did not choose to hold on. She was simply the one standing there, and the lamp
> does not ask permission. […] Her mentor is not a memory. She is a voice, inside
> the lamp, still working.

## Readability needs

- Must still read, at gameplay zoom, as the class's signature silhouette: bare
  head (no hood/helm — carries over, now for the same in-world reason plus her own
  read), the carried point of light at hip/staff height, the palette-shift radius
  doing most of the visual work. That silhouette language
  (`../art/noll.md` §3, unchanged) is **not** retired — only the face/age/gender/
  backstory grounding is.
- Portrait needs to communicate "young, not a child, not old" clearly — she is
  visually distinct from both the retired 28-year-old-male read and from the
  Relickeeper's age register (64) at the opposite end of the hero row.
- The lamp remains the single most important object in her kit narratively (it is
  literally her mentor) — if the pixel-art-director wants to give the lamp any
  additional visual weight/distinctness beyond the standard Watch-Lamp bright to
  reflect this, that's a valid design question to raise back to narrative, not
  assumed here.
- No new visual asset is needed for "the mentor's voice" itself — it is a bark/dialogue
  system concern, not a sprite. Flagging only so it isn't mistaken for a missing
  visual subject.

## Subject
The stairwell landing Warden-Captain Bree has held for years: a barricade of Legion
shields turned inward, sigils scoured off, one lamp above. Flag N1 gives it three
persistent looks across runs — **held** (her watch / permanent safe room), **unheld**
(she was recruited; lamp gone, barricade abandoned), and **fallen** (a Legion
strongpoint built over her post).

## Mood
Held: one honest light in an occupied city — small, permanent, defended. Unheld:
recent absence, a watch that ended. Fallen: administrative-cold — the Legion has
filed her stand away and re-occupied it.

## Narrative excerpt
> A barricade built of Legion shields turned inward, every one scoured clean of the
> crown sigil, and above them a single lamp — the only honest light the party has
> seen since the gates.

## Readability needs
- The **held/safe-room state must read as safe before the player commits** —
  approaching at speed, from any stair direction, in an otherwise hostile floor.
- The **fallen state must read as hostile strongpoint**, never as the safe room —
  these two states must be unconfusable at gameplay zoom.
- The unheld state should read as *emptied*, not destroyed (she left; she didn't lose).

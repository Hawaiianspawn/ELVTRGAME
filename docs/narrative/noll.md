# The Lampbearer (v1 hero)
Extends: `CLASSES.md` §4 (Lampbearer — the Guided, Kindle, Sanctuary, decision hooks); `WORLD.md` §3c (soul-flames, the Unlit, snuffers), §5 (the Last Lamplighter), §8 (E2/E5/E6/E7); `GDD.md` §2 (tone). Class names are working names; per the 2026-07-11 no-names decision, every playable class is referred to by role only — no invented individual name. This file replaces the retired "Noll" fiction (a foundling who arrived carrying his own lit lamp) with a new origin per owner decision (see Canon proposals). She. Her mentor is referred to by relationship only, never by name, per the same decision — the mentor is a WORLD.md-style NPC-adjacent figure but is deliberately kept unnamed and off the §7 flag budget (see proposals).

The Lampbearer does not carry her own lamp. She carries her mentor's — the one
who trained her, worked the Vesper Halls for decades before her, and died with
that lamp in reach. When it happened, the lamp did what a Lampbearer's lamp
always does: it found the nearest unclaimed soul-flame and caught it, before
anything worse could. The nearest flame was her mentor's own, rising off a
body that hadn't finished falling. The Lampbearer did not choose to hold on.
She was simply the one standing there, and the lamp does not ask permission.

Her mentor is not a memory. She is a voice, inside the lamp, still working.

## History

In the Undervault, the dead rise as a small floating flame — a soul-flame,
unclaimed, briefly its own — and the dark does not let a thing like that sit
unclaimed for long. Left too long, a flame turns hostile on its own (the Unlit)
or is simply eaten (a snuffer's whole reason for existing). A Lampbearer's work
is a race against both outcomes, one flame at a time, and her mentor had run
that race for longer than the Lampbearer had been alive.

She was not the mentor's apprentice in the way the word usually means — no one
chooses a Lampbearer, and no Lampbearer chooses her replacement. The role
passes by whoever's nearest when an old one goes out. She was the mentor's
attendant: carried oil, minded braziers, learned the Vesper Halls' dead ends
by lantern-light while her knees were young enough not to complain about the
stairs. She was not being groomed. She was being useful.

The night it happened, deep in a lamp-hall long since gone dark, a snuffer had
already found the mentor before the mentor found it — a bad wound, no time,
and a thing in the room built for exactly one purpose: eat whatever rose next.
The mentor's last working act was not a lesson. It was shoving the lamp into
the nearest hands, hers, an instant before her own flame left her body — not a
blessing, not a choice made with love and foresight, just the last reflex of
someone who had spent a lifetime making sure nothing got left for the dark to
take. The lamp caught the flame a half-breath before the snuffer did. The
Lampbearer has turned that half-breath over in her head every day since. She
does not know if she was chosen or simply *there*. She has decided it doesn't
matter, out loud, often enough that everyone at the Gatecamp knows she doesn't
quite believe it.

## The face

Mid-twenties, features gone quietly severe from Vesper Halls damp and years of
bad light — the pallor of someone who reads by lamp more than sun. She wears
the mentor's old tender's wrap without having earned the rank it once implied,
and no one has told her to stop. Steady hands, burn-flecked from wick-work she
was doing years before it was hers to do alone. Her stillness reads as calm
until you notice what she's actually doing: listening, always, to the lamp at
her hip, the way a daughter half-listens for a parent in the next room.

## The light: the Borrowed Lamp

The Lampbearer's reserved bright is the **lamp**, and hers has never been
relit, because she was never the one who lit it. It holds her mentor's flame
the way it holds every flame she catches below — the same mechanism, the same
warmth, no difference in the tool between a stranger's soul and the person who
raised her. That fact is either a comfort or an unbearable one, depending on
the day. Every brazier and beacon she rekindles in the Undervault is lit, in
some literal sense, by the same fire her mentor is still burning inside — so
every relit hall is her mentor's work continuing, hand after hand removed.
Art: the lamp renders lit and upright in every state, including hero-down, per
the one-flame rule (shared visual grammar with every honest lamp in the game).

## Voice

Two voices, not one — the Lampbearer's own, young and still finding its
authority; and her mentor's, from inside the lamp, dry with decades of exactly
this work and now with nothing left to do but talk her through it.

1. *(Lampbearer, finding a soul)* "There you are. No hurry now."
2. *(the mentor, same moment)* "Faster than that. It's not going to wait for you to be gentle. Gentle comes after."
3. *(Lampbearer, soothing an Unlit, E2)* "You don't have to be what the quiet made you. I've got you. Look at the lamp."
4. *(the mentor, unprompted, mid-fight)* "You're doing it right. I wouldn't tell you if you weren't."
5. *(the mentor, quieter, off to the side of a fight)* "You can let me go, when you're ready. I mean that. I've meant it every time I've said it."
6. *(Lampbearer, changing the subject)* "Watch your flank."

## The Guided

Captured soul-flames, every one — the dead of the Undervault, found before the
dark could claim them, drifting in her wake as a living constellation. She
greets each light when it joins and greets it again when it re-kindles after
guttering, and she does not pretend they are the people they were. What's left
is owed gentleness regardless, and gentleness is a discipline she was taught
by the same voice that now rides at her hip, correcting her technique in
real time. Her mentor is not one of the Guided. Her mentor is the lamp itself —
the thing that does the catching, not one of the caught, and the distinction
matters to the Lampbearer more than she can explain to anyone who asks.

## Decision hooks

- **E6 · The Lost's Plea (her event):** gathered souls beg for rest at a
  shrine. She always lets them go — the discipline holds for strangers,
  every time, without exception. It is the one soul who has *earned* the
  right to ask, and asks it plainly, that she has never once released. The
  event's teeth, for her specifically: watching her keep the rule for
  everyone except the person who taught it to her.
- **E2 · Soothe or Strike:** every Unlit she meets is a flame someone didn't
  reach in time — a hair's width from what her mentor would have become if
  the lamp had been a half-breath slower. She soothes under fire, at cost,
  past sense, and the party's job is deciding when they can afford her
  mercy, because she has already decided.
- **E5 · The Rescue Site (Lampbearer-flavored):** survivors huddled where the
  light died, guttering. Her mentor talks her through it in real time — old
  habits, old shortcuts, occasionally a correction that arrives a beat too
  late to matter, which neither of them mentions.
- **E7 · The Dark Bargain (private):** the whisper offers her the one thing
  she has never let herself ask for — to free her mentor without having to
  be the one who decides it. Quietly. Cleanly. No goodbye, no choosing, no
  guilt either way. It does not say what "freed" would actually mean, or
  what it would cost the lamp, or her. She has not taken it. She has also
  never told her mentor it was offered.

## At the Gatecamp

She keeps the Vesper Halls watch-board — whose flame is whose, which halls
still owe a rescue — the same board her mentor kept, in the same hand her
mentor taught her, slowly turning into her own. As Souls at Rest rises (F2),
the Gatecamp's nights go quiet, and she is the first to notice each haunt that
doesn't come back, which she marks in the vigil book without comment. She sits
with the dying when asked, the way her mentor used to, and says the same
things, in the same order, and has never once decided whether that's
inheritance or just not having anything better to say.

## Canon proposals

1. **Retire the "Noll" identity in CLASSES.md §4:** the Lampbearer's "who/
   face/light" bullets currently describe a foundling who arrived at the
   Gatecamp as a child already carrying a lit lamp — that origin is retired
   per the owner's 2026-07-11 decision. Replace with a summary of this file:
   she carries her mentor's lamp, not her own; her mentor's soul is captured
   inside it, aware and speaking; the capture was involuntary, a reflex at
   the moment of death, not a chosen succession. Also strip the roster
   table's "Hero" row and the "Writing convention" paragraph, per the same
   decision (see hallam.md/edda.md proposals for the identical ask).
2. **Retinue description tightened (CLASSES.md §4, "Retinue: the Guided"):**
   the current text calls the Guided "lost souls, candle-wisps, and stray
   survivors too broken to fight" — under the new soul-flame cosmology (see
   WORLD.md §3c edit, this pass) the Guided should read unambiguously as
   *captured soul-flames*, full stop, rather than a mix that implies some
   are still-living rescued survivors. Suggested replacement clause:
   "captured soul-flames — the newly dead of the Undervault, caught before
   the dark claims them." If the design still wants a separate category of
   rescued *living* stragglers in the Lampbearer's kit, that should be its
   own line, not folded into "the Guided."
3. **Deliberately no new WORLD.md NPC:** the mentor is written as a
   WORLD.md-caliber figure (named role, personal history, an ongoing voice)
   but the §7 flag budget is explicitly locked at 15 with no room to add
   without cutting something (§7 budget check). This file keeps her unnamed
   and off the NPC list on purpose. Flagging as an open fork: if the owner
   wants her promoted to a numbered NPC (N6) with her own flag and E8 hook,
   something else in the 15 has to go first.
4. **Open fork — does the mentor ever get released?** Left genuinely
   unresolved by design, on purpose (recombinant per-run texture, not a
   scripted arc) — but flagging it explicitly since it's the emotional spine
   of the class. If a future content pass wants a capstone resolution (a
   late-game event where the Lampbearer's own E6 finally applies to her
   mentor), it should be authored as an E6 variant, not a new template.

# Warden-Captain Bree — barks, first meeting, and the N1 decision
Extends: `WORLD.md` §5 (NPC seed 1), §7 (flag N1), §8 (template E8); `CLASSES.md` (Vanguard, Shield Wall). All names are working names.

She is the last officer of the true Legion — the army that *wasn't* taken. For years
she has held one stairwell in the Highgates against her own hollowed comrades, and
she still calls it a watch, not a war. Everything she says is procedure said like a
prayer.

## Ambient barks (at her post, flag N1 = met, before the decision)

Triggered on proximity/idle while the party shares her landing. One line, no camera.

1. "Third watch. Post held. Same as I'll write tomorrow."
2. "The pikeman on the left stair — I swore him in myself. If you have to put him down, do it clean."
3. "They keep sending the muster up. I keep sending it back. One of us will run out of years."

## First meeting (vignette — flag N1: unmet → met)

The stairwell announces itself before she does: a barricade built of Legion shields
turned inward, every one scoured clean of the crown sigil, and above them a single
lamp — the only honest light the party has seen since the gates. A voice from behind
the wall gives the old challenge-word of the Gatewatch, waits exactly three breaths
for a countersign no living person remembers, and then answers it herself, quietly,
the way you finish a dead friend's sentence. When she steps into the lamplight she
is smaller than the legend the Gatecamp tells — grey at the temples, armor patched
with kitchen tin — and she looks past the heroes, down the dark stair, out of habit,
before she looks at them. "You came down the long way," she says. "Good. That means
my stair still isn't the easy one."

## The decision — The Keeper's Ask (E8, moves flag N1)

- **Trigger site:** her held landing in the Highgates (generates while N1 = met).
- **Owner:** one player (per GDD §6 — the party sees the choice, one player answers).

Bree makes the ask herself, and she refuses to make it easy: *"I can march with you,
or you can stand a watch with me. I won't pretend both keep the stair."*

**Option A — "March with me." (power now)**
- **Run effect:** Bree joins as an elite NPC — a Vanguard-style anchor. She holds a
  one-woman Shield Wall (blocks enemy pathing like the stance) and nearby Liberated
  hold formation under fear as if Veterans.
- **Flag effect:** N1 → **recruited**. The lamp comes down with her; the stairwell
  generates unheld in future runs. If she falls this run, N1 → **fallen**: her post
  spawns as a Legion strongpoint ever after.

**Option B — "Stand the watch." (a healed world)**
- **Run effect:** a hold-the-line defense event *now* — the floor's muster comes to
  take the stair while the party holds it beside her. Costs the run time, resources,
  and blood; yields no recruit.
- **Flag effect:** on success, N1 → **post held**: the stairwell becomes a permanent
  safe room in every future run (per §7). If the line breaks and she dies, N1 →
  **fallen**, as above.

Teeth check: A is the best formation unit a run can get, and it can end in her death
on some floor she never swore to. B costs a hard fight for zero run power — and buys
the world one small, permanent light.

SKILL.md: straddle and shieldout each open their gap to the sprite's edge (0 holes); akimbo and overhead close it (2 holes each, 10px and 19px -- the akimbo gap is near the floor a downsample can still show). Every variant appears as rendered and again as a flat outline, because the outline is what survives at panel scale. All figures share one zoom, so relative size is true.

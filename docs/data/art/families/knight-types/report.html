Unit-role variants (bulwark, pikeman, skirmisher) at the same base pose, moved narrow <-> wide by tucking/extending limbs and gear rather than by proportion knobs. Every variant appears as rendered and again as a flat outline, because the outline is what survives at panel scale. All figures share one zoom, so relative size is true.

archer-medieval -- variant family

Two variants move width via the skeletal-base axis SKILL.md proves works -- narrow (arms tucked, bow slung on the back) versus wide-by-extension (a fully drawn longbow held out at arm's length) -- never tall<->squat, since knight-melee-v1 measured this base will not compress vertically. Four variants move topology instead, each opening or closing a different silhouette shape while staying a ranged unit: a single raised loosing arm enclosing a hole against the head/shoulder (the v13_maceraised recipe), a quiver held away from the torso enclosing a hole on every side (SKILL.md's 'hold an object away from the body' rule), a forward crouch bracing a crossbow low and level (a genuinely lower, more compact profile than the upright bow poses), and a sling whirled overhead in a wide arc (a loop of open air well clear of the body). Every variant appears as rendered and again as a flat outline, because the outline is what survives at panel scale. All figures share one zoom, so relative size is true.

knight-primitive -- variant family

Each state strips the knight to one primitive shape (square, triangle, dome, column, inverted triangle, bar) with the weapon stowed and limbs tucked -- pure ratio and solidity change, no topology move. Every variant appears as rendered and again as a flat outline, because the outline is what survives at panel scale. All figures share one zoom, so relative size is true.

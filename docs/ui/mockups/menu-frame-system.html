What to look at: the woodcut reference becomes The Sampler Frame — beast lintel + blackletter crest, two flanking guardian panels, four corner rosette medallions, footer input band. The reference's empty middle is repurposed as the central well: an RTS company readout — aggregate company meter on top, then square squad cards (Warcraft-format). Each card's health is its rank-and-file muster: one pip per soldier (a file at scale), lit = standing, dark = fallen — so a squad's count, formation, health and scale are one read. The wide card is a 50-strong compacted squad (10 files × 5 ranks). The Follow / Charge / Hold / Rally wheel and leash gauge stay on the right. Strictly the four locked Demichrome values; 1px halftone on static bands only. Dragon and guardian figures are placeholder plates — real art is an art brief to the pixel-art-director.

OrthoWidth 2400 only contains a spawn ring out to ~1386uu — the sideways swing of the 120° arc binds before depth does. Against the real ring of 2500–4000uu, that's an escape of ~2614uu at the ring's outer edge. Every dot outside the box is a soldier the player cannot see arrive.

Recommendation — D, fit-to-bbox with named clamps

Widen and tighten are both a fixed tax paid every frame, even in a lull with three brood left on screen. Fit-to-bbox only pays A's soldier-size cost while a wave is actually big enough to need it, and it is the only option that touches zero gameplay CVars — BroodSpawnRadiusMin/Max stay exactly where systems tuned them. It is also the first concrete step toward docs/design/CAMERA-SCALE.md's "army or a man" premise: a camera already driven by live population, ready to extend toward the lone-survivor shot that doc asks for.
